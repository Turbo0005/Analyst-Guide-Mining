Lithium

Battery raw material, with a brutal boom-bust cycle since 2021.

The market

  • Supply: WA hard rock (Greenbushes, Pilgangoora, Mt Holland, Kathleen Valley), South American brines, Chinese lepidolite and African pegmatites.
  • Processing: concentrates go to converters (mostly in China) to make carbonate or hydroxide.
  • Cycle: Core Lithium suspended mining (5 Jan 2024), PLS put Ngungaju into care and maintenance (effective 1 Dec 2024) and Liontown raised at a 35.5% discount after a failed bid (Oct 2023) ✔.
  • Demand: EV and grid-storage batteries.
⚑ Red flagWhat to watch
  • Always normalise grade (SC6) and delivery terms (FOB/CIF).
  • DLE has a poor record of reaching commercial scale.
  • Check offtake pricing mechanisms (floors, caps).
