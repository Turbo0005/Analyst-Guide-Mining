Commodity one-pager

Graphite

Battery anode material, with a supply chain dominated by China.

On this page
  1. At a glance
  2. The market
  3. Where it is taught

At a glance

ProductsFlake concentrate → spherical purified graphite (SPG) → anode; synthetic graphite competes
PricedContracts and price-reporting agencies
SupplyChina dominates processing; Africa (Mozambique, Tanzania) for flake

The market

  • Demand: lithium-ion battery anodes.
  • Challenges: qualification with customers, purification, and competition from synthetic graphite.
  • Policy: Chinese export controls and Western incentives.
⚑ Red flagWhat to watch
  • Flake size distribution and purity.
  • Downstream (SPG) execution risk and offtakes.
