Gold

The monetary metal, and the ASX's largest resources sector by number of companies.

The market

  • Demand: central banks, ETFs, bars and coins, jewellery (India and China), a little technology.
  • Drivers: real interest rates, the US dollar, central-bank buying, fear and fiscal worries.
  • Supply: mine output plus recycling, with no dominant country. Australia is a top producer.
  • Equities: producers trade on A$ margin and reserve life; explorers need risk appetite as well as price.
⚑ Red flagWhat to watch
  • A$ gold, not US$ gold, drives WA producers' margins (Module 12).
  • Specify the price series (spot intraday vs LBMA PM).
  • Watch for hedge books capping upside (Module 19).
