From good ideas to a good portfolio

You can be right on half your picks and still lose money if the losers are oversized, illiquid or all exposed to the same commodity. Portfolio construction turns analysis into returns.

Core and satellites

  • Core (60–80%): diversified majors, mid-tier producers and royalty companies. They have cash flow, liquidity and survivability.
  • Satellites (20–40%): developers and explorers. They offer asymmetric upside and binary risk.

Stage-based caps instead of Kelly maths

The Kelly criterion needs accurate win probabilities and payoffs, which you do not have for a junior explorer. Use simple stage caps:

Stage Max position (% of portfolio) unverified
Diversified major 8%
Royalty / streaming 8%
Mid-tier producer 6%
Developer 3%
Explorer 1–2%
◆ Key ideaSize for the drawdown, not the dream

An explorer can fall 70–90% on a failed hole or a broken raise. At a 1.5% position, a 90% loss costs your portfolio 1.35%. At 10%, it costs 9%. Base rates (Module 2) say most explorers fail. Size accordingly.

Liquidity-aware sizing and correlation

  • Cap each position so you can exit in a few days at normal volume (Module 20).
  • Correlation: five WA gold developers are one bet on A$ gold and WA costs, not five bets. Cap commodity and jurisdiction exposure as well as single names.

Selling and trimming into strength

Most investors have a buying process and no selling process. Commodity equities punish that.

∑ Key formulaA simple trimming playbook
  1. Thesis-based exits: sell when the reason you bought has played out (the discovery is resourced, the study is delivered, the bid is announced) or is broken.
  2. Valuation trims: trim when the implied price exceeds your long-term deck by more than 20–30%.
  3. Size trims: trim back to the cap when a winner outgrows it.
  4. Time stops: reassess if nothing happens in 12–18 months.
▣ Case studyThe 2026 gold drawdown ✔

Gold peaked at ≈US$5,595/oz intraday on 29 Jan 2026 and was ≈US$4,050 by 29 Oct 2026, about −28%. Investors who trimmed gold producers when implied prices ran far above long-term decks protected gains. Those who added at the peak learned that operating leverage works both ways (Module 1).

Tax: CGT and franking (Australia)

  • CGT discount: individuals holding an asset for more than 12 months get a 50% discount on the capital gain. Consider that when trimming near the 12-month mark.
  • Tax-loss harvesting: realise losses before 30 June to offset gains (watch the wash-sale rules).
  • Franking credits: dividends from Australian producers that pay company tax come with credits. Royalty and major dividends are often fully franked.

The decision journal

For every buy, sell and pass, record: date, price, thesis, the implied price, the key risks, your confidence, and what would change your mind. Review it every quarter. It is the only reliable way to learn from your own decisions and to measure your calibration (as the game does with Brier scores).
