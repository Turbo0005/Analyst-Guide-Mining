The task

Choose a live ASX developer or mid-tier producer, ideally one with a TSX or LSE peer for comparison. Produce a full investment thesis using every tool in this guide. Budget 15–25 hours.

The nine deliverables

# Deliverable Modules Tool
1 Commodity view: supply, demand, cost curve, your deck vs consensus 12–13 A$ gold / one-pagers
2 DCF/NAV with a tornado chart and the implied-price calculation 16–17 NPV model
3 Resource and reserve critique: dates, cut-offs, conversion, Inferred share 5–6 Grade-tonnage, funnel
4 Funding and dilution forecast: headline vs fully funded NAV/share 14 Funding calculator
5 Hedge, royalty and stream deductions in the NAV 19 Hedge MTM
6 Jurisdiction, ESG and management scorecards 21–22 Scorecards
7 Catalyst calendar for 12 months 26 Tear sheet
8 Bull, base and bear cases with probabilities 26 Scenario EV
9 Position size, exit rules and a one-page pitch 27 Sizing

Rubric (9 deliverables × 4 levels)

Level Description
1 · Novice Reports company numbers without checking dates or assumptions
2 · Competent Rebuilds key numbers; identifies main risks; uses a single price deck
3 · Proficient Three decks, an implied price, a fully funded NAV, explicit cash-flow risk adjustments, a dated source for every number
4 · Expert Variant perception grounded in base rates; a pre-mortem; a position size justified by liquidity and drawdown; monitoring triggers

Aim for level 3 on every deliverable and level 4 on at least three.

✎ Your turnMini-capstone (fast track)

If you took the fast track: produce a tear sheet plus an implied-price calculation for one company. Use the NPV model with the company's own study inputs in place of Hemi's.

Revisit at 6 and 12 months

Set calendar reminders. At each revisit:

  1. What happened versus your base case?
  2. Which assumptions were wrong, and were they knowable?
  3. What was your confidence, and what is your Brier-style score on the key calls?
  4. Would you still own it at today's price?
