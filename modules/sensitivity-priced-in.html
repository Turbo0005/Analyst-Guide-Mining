The most useful skill in this guide

Anyone can build a DCF at a chosen price and declare a stock "cheap". The professional move is to run it backwards: what commodity price is the market already paying for? If the implied price is well below your view (and the futures curve), the stock may be cheap. If it is above spot, the market is already pricing in optimism.

Tornado charts

A tornado chart flexes each input up and down by a set amount and ranks the effect on NPV. For almost every mine, the order is:

  1. Commodity price (and FX, for Australian producers)
  2. Grade and recovery (they act like price, since revenue ∝ grade × recovery × price)
  3. Operating cost
  4. Capex (bigger for developers, smaller for producers)
  5. Discount rate and delay
◆ Key ideaRead the asymmetry

Price, grade and recovery are multiplicative on revenue, while costs subtract. When the margin is thin, revenue sensitivities explode (operating leverage again). When the margin is fat, as in Hemi at spot, capex and delay become relatively less important, and price dominates.

Break-even price

The break-even price is the commodity price at which NPV = 0. For the Hemi DFS reconstruction, post-tax NPV5 = 0 at about A$1,650/oz (model output). At ≈A$5,750 spot as of 29 Oct 2026, that is a very wide safety margin, before cost inflation.

The implied price: running the model backwards

∑ Key formulaImplied price

Find price P* such that *NPV(P) × risk factor + other NAV items = market value (or bid value)

Then compare P* with spot, consensus and the futures curve.

◎ Worked exampleWorked: what gold price did NST pay for?

NST paid about A$5bn for De Grey (completed 5 May 2025) ✔. Ignore De Grey's cash and other assets, and assume the price bought Hemi's DFS case outright (risk factor 1.0):

  • With DFS costs and capex, the model gives post-tax NPV5 = A$5bn at about A$3,440/oz.
  • With AISC +30% and capex +25%, it gives about A$3,920/oz.

Compare that with the A$ gold price on the bid date (look up the Perth Mint history for Dec 2024), and with today's ≈A$5,750. NST paid in its own shares, which also move with gold. The implied price sits far below today's spot, so NST was not paying for spot gold. It was paying for a conservative gold price plus optionality on the resource beyond the 6.0 Moz reserve, which left the subsequent gold rally as upside for NST holders.

Find the implied price if NST had applied a 0.8 risk factor to Hemi (i.e. it paid A$5bn for 0.8 × NPV). (Hint: you need NPV = A$6.25bn. Use the slider until post-tax NPV ≈ 6,250.)

NAV at three decks

Always show NAV at three price decks:

  • Spot (what the market sees today)
  • Consensus (what brokers assume, usually a declining long-term curve)
  • Long-term / conservative (a mid-cycle price)

If a stock only looks cheap at spot, it is a leveraged bet on spot persisting.

Standard stress tests

◆ Key ideaRun these every time
  1. 12-month delay to first production (permitting, funding, construction).
  2. 25% capex overrun.
  3. AISC +20% (inflation, lower grade).
  4. Price −20% against your base deck.

At the DFS deck, Hemi's model loses about 6% of post-tax NPV from a one-year delay (A$2.93bn → A$2.75bn) and about 8% from a 25% overrun (→ A$2.69bn). Both are small next to a 20% price move.

"Priced in"

▣ Case studyFirst Quantum and Cobre Panamá ✔

On 28 Nov 2023, Panama's Supreme Court declared Cobre Panamá's contract unconstitutional, the worst-case outcome. First Quantum fell as much as 8.3% intraday and pared most of the loss. Why so small? More than C$10bn of value had already been wiped out during weeks of protests. The market had already priced the bad scenario. The ruling only confirmed it.

✦ Hemi dossierPage 15: What was priced into DEG?
  • Pre-bid close: A$1.52 (29 Nov 2024). Offer: A$2.08 (37.1% premium). Closing price on bid day: A$1.97.
  • Implied gold price in the ≈A$5bn deal value: ≈A$3,400–3,900/oz on DFS-to-stressed costs (model).
  • Before the bid, DEG's market value was lower still, so it implied an even lower gold price or a big funding and execution discount. That gap was the opportunity a bidder could capture.
  • Takeaway: the market was pricing a funding gap and ramp-up risk, not the geology.
⚑ Red flagSensitivity red flags
  • Studies showing only upside sensitivities
  • Tornado charts with asymmetric ranges chosen to flatter
  • Valuations quoted only at spot
  • No delay scenario for an unpermitted project
