Learning from the whole distribution

Famous winners teach survivorship bias. This workshop deliberately mixes booms, busts and quiet compounders. Each case is a decision point: what did you know, what could you have inferred, and what happened next?

◆ Key ideaHow to run a case
  1. Read only the information dated before the decision point.
  2. Write your call: direction, magnitude and confidence.
  3. Reveal what happened, and score yourself (the game does this with Brier scores).
  4. Write a one-line lesson in your decision journal.

The lithium cycle: PLS

  • Boom: spodumene prices spiked in 2022. PLS's FY23 average realised price was US$4,447/dmt (CIF, ≈SC5.3) ✔.
  • Bust: by the June 2024 quarter, PLS's realised price was US$840/t against an FOB cost of US$591/t ✔.
  • Response: the "P850" operating model and Ngungaju placed in care and maintenance, announced 29 Oct 2024 and effective 1 Dec 2024 ✔. The company was renamed PLS Group in Nov 2025, a separate event ✔.
  • Decision point: at the 2022 peak, what did a producer's P/NAV at spot imply about the long-term price? (Answer: a price far above the cost curve's incentive level, an unsustainable assumption.)

The Chalice round trip

16c → about 50c in a week (Mar 2020) → about A$4.44 (2020) → about A$10.48 (Nov 2021) → about A$0.94 (Oct 2026) ✔. Decision point: after the maiden Gonneville resource (Nov 2021, 330 Mt @ 0.58% NiEq, only ~0.16% Ni) ✔, which questions should have tempered enthusiasm? (By-product dependence, PGM price risk, scale of capex, metallurgy.)

Core Lithium: suspension

On 5 Jan 2024, Core suspended mining at Finniss and moved to processing about 280 kt of stockpiles. The shares fell 11.5% that day ✔. Decision point: why so little? (The price collapse was already priced in.)

Paladin: a restart and a merger

  • Restart: Langer Heinrich declared commercial production on 30 Mar 2024 ✔.
  • Merger: the Fission acquisition completed 23 Dec 2024, Fission was delisted on 24 Dec, and PDN began trading on the TSX on 27 Dec 2024 ✔. It brought Patterson Lake South (Triple R), a basement-hosted, unconformity-related uranium deposit in Canada's Athabasca region.
  • Decision point: restarts are "second ramp-ups". What risks repeat? (Ramp-up, recovery, contract pricing.)

Kamoa-Kakula: world-class and still exposed

Normal feed ≈4.9–5.1% Cu. After the May 2025 seismic event, the mill ran at 3.3% Cu and 79% recovery in June 2025 on stockpiles ✔. Guidance was cut from 520–580 kt to 370–420 kt, and IVN fell 16.19% on 26 May 2025 ✔.

Lynas: the strategic ex-China supplier

Lynas began producing separated dysprosium in May 2025 ✔, described as the first commercial separated heavy rare-earth production outside China unverified. Its history shows strategic value, policy support and the processing risk of rare earths. unverified
