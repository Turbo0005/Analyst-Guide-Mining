How it works

Rate each card Again (back to box 1), Hard (stay in the same box) or Good (move up a box). Cards come due when their box interval has passed. Keyboard shortcuts: Space flips the card, and 1 / 2 / 3 rate it. Use the module filter to drill one module, or link to flashcards.html?m=module-slug.
