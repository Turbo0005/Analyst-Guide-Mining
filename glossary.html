Glossary

157 terms. Dotted-underlined terms anywhere in the guide show these definitions on hover or tap.

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTVW

A

AACE class
Cost-estimate classification (Class 5 = scoping … Class 3 = feasibility) used to describe study accuracy.
AIC
All-in Cost: AISC plus non-sustaining (growth) capital, greenfield exploration and studies.
Aircore
Cheap, fast, shallow drilling through soft cover and weathered rock; used for first-pass geochemistry (how Hemi was found).
AISC
All-in Sustaining Cost (World Gold Council): site costs + royalties + sustaining capex + corporate G&A and more, per ounce sold.
Alteration
Chemical change of host rock by mineralising fluids; forms halos larger than the ore that guide exploration.
Appendix 5B
The quarterly cash-flow report lodged by ASX mining exploration entities; section 8 shows funded quarters.
Arbitrage spread
The gap between a takeover offer's value and the target's share price; prices time and deal risk.
Assay
Laboratory analysis of a sample's metal content.
Aware letter
An ASX letter asking when a company became aware of information, i.e. whether disclosure was timely.

B

Banded iron formation
Layered Precambrian iron-rich sedimentary rock; the source of Pilbara iron ore.
BIF
Banded iron formation.
BIOX
Bacterial oxidation of sulphide concentrates to free refractory gold.
Block caving
Underground method that undercuts a massive orebody so it collapses under gravity; low cost per tonne, huge capex.
Bookbuild
The process where a lead manager gathers institutional bids for a placement, usually during a trading halt.
Break-even price
The commodity price at which a project's NPV equals zero.
Brier score
A measure of probabilistic forecast accuracy: the mean squared error of probabilities (0 = perfect).
By-product credit
Revenue from secondary metals deducted from costs (can make C1 negative).

C

C1 cash cost
Direct site cash costs (mining, processing, G&A, TC/RC, freight) net of by-product credits; excludes royalties and sustaining capex.
Capital intensity
Capex divided by annual production (e.g. A$ per annual ounce).
Capitalised stripping
Waste removal costs recorded as an asset rather than an expense, flattering current costs.
Care and maintenance
A mine that is shut but kept ready for restart.
CIF
Cost, insurance and freight: delivered-port price including shipping.
CIL
Carbon-in-leach: gold dissolved by cyanide is adsorbed onto activated carbon in the same tanks.
CIP
Carbon-in-pulp: like CIL but with leaching and adsorption in separate stages.
Collar
The surface location where a drill hole starts.
Collar table
A table of drill-hole locations, azimuths, dips and depths, required in JORC releases.
Comminution
Crushing and grinding ore; usually the largest energy cost in processing.
Competent Person
The qualified, accountable professional who signs off JORC estimates and results.
Concentrate
The product of flotation: a mineral-rich powder sold to smelters.
Contained metal
Tonnes × grade: metal in the ground before any losses.
Cost curve
Producers ranked by unit cost with bar width = production; shows the marginal producer.
CRIRSCO
The international committee aligning reporting codes (JORC, NI 43-101, S-K 1300 and others).
Cut-off grade
The grade above which material is reported as resource or treated as ore.

D

DCF
Discounted cash flow: present value of future cash flows.
DFS
Definitive (or bankable) feasibility study, typically ±10–15% accuracy; basis for financing and FID.
Diamond drilling
Drilling that recovers solid core; best for structure, metallurgy and density, but most expensive.
Dilution
(1) Waste mixed into ore during mining. (2) Reduction of shareholders' percentage by issuing new shares.
DLE
Direct lithium extraction from brines; a technology with a poor record of reaching commercial scale.
DMS
Dense media separation: gravity separation used for spodumene.
Doré
Unrefined gold-silver bars poured at a mine site.
DSO
Direct-shipping ore: iron ore that needs only crushing and screening.

E

EBITDA
Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation.
EIA
Environmental impact assessment.
Electrowinning
Recovering metal from solution using electric current (gold from eluate; copper in SX-EW).
Entitlement offer
A pro-rata rights issue to all existing shareholders.
EPA
Environmental Protection Authority (WA approves major projects).
EPBC
Australia's federal Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act.
EPC
Engineer–procure–construct: a contractor builds for a (largely) fixed price and bears more overrun risk.
EPCM
Engineering, procurement and construction management: the owner bears overrun risk.
Escrow
A lock-up of shares (often vendor or IPO shares) for a set period.
EV
Enterprise value: market cap + debt − cash (+ other claims).
EV/oz
Enterprise value per ounce of resource or reserve; a crude multiple for gold companies.
Exploration Target
A range of tonnes and grade without a resource; must carry a cautionary statement.

F

FCF yield
Free cash flow divided by market capitalisation.
FID
Final investment decision: the board's commitment to build.
FIRB
Foreign Investment Review Board.
Flotation
Separating sulphide minerals by attaching them to air bubbles in a froth.
FOB
Free on board: price at the loading port, excluding freight.
Forward sale
A hedge locking in a future selling price.
Fully funded NAV
NAV per share after the dilution required to fund a project.
Funding gap
Capex and other pre-production needs minus debt capacity and cash.

G

GISTM
Global Industry Standard on Tailings Management.
Grade
Concentration of metal in rock (g/t for gold, % for base metals).
Grade control
Close-spaced drilling or sampling during mining to separate ore from waste.
Gram-metre
Width × grade (g/t × m), a quick measure of an intercept's metal content.
Greenstone belt
Archaean volcanic-sedimentary belts hosting much of the world's orogenic gold and nickel.

H

Hedge book
A company's outstanding hedges (forwards, options).
HPAL
High-pressure acid leach for limonite nickel laterites; notoriously hard to ramp up.

I

IER
Independent Expert's Report on a takeover's fairness, with a valuation.
Including
A higher-grade sub-interval reported within a longer intercept; not additional metal.
Indicated Resource
A resource with reasonable confidence; can convert to a Probable Reserve.
Inferred Resource
A resource with low confidence; cannot be converted to a Reserve.
Infill drilling
Drilling between existing holes to increase confidence.
Intrusion-hosted gold
Gold mineralisation hosted within an intrusive rock body, like Hemi's diorites.
IOCG
Iron oxide copper-gold deposits (Olympic Dam, Prominent Hill).
IP
Induced polarisation: a geophysical method detecting disseminated sulphides.
IRR
Internal rate of return: the discount rate at which NPV = 0.
ISR
In-situ recovery: pumping solutions through permeable ore to dissolve metal (uranium).

J

JORC Code
The Australasian code for reporting exploration results, resources and reserves.
Junior
A small exploration or development company.

K

koz
Thousand troy ounces.

L

Lassonde curve
The typical value path of a discovery from exploration to production.
LCE
Lithium carbonate equivalent (Li × 5.323).
Lead manager
The broker running a placement.
LME
London Metal Exchange.
LR 5.23
ASX rule allowing re-quotation of earlier estimates if not aware of material new information.
LR 7.1
ASX rule limiting issues to 15% of capital in 12 months without approval (7.1A adds 10%).

M

Mark-to-market
Value of hedges at current prices; negative = revenue forgone.
Measured Resource
The highest-confidence resource; can convert to a Proved Reserve.
Metal equivalent
Converting several metals into one grade using assumed prices and recoveries.
MHP
Mixed hydroxide precipitate, an intermediate nickel-cobalt product from HPAL.
Modifying factors
Mining, processing, economic, legal, environmental, social and governmental factors that convert resources to reserves.
Moz
Million troy ounces.
Mtpa
Million tonnes per annum.

N

Nameplate
The design capacity of a plant.
NAV
Net asset value: sum of asset NPVs plus net cash minus corporate costs and other adjustments.
NI 43-101
Canada's mineral disclosure standard, in force Feb 2001 after Bre-X.
NPI
(1) Net profit interest royalty. (2) Nickel pig iron.
NPV
Net present value of a project's cash flows.
NSR
Net smelter return: revenue after smelting, refining, transport and payability (also a royalty type).
Nugget effect
Erratic grade distribution from coarse gold; repeat assays vary widely.

O

Offtake
An agreement to sell future production to a buyer.
Operating leverage
Sensitivity of margin to price: %Δmargin = %Δprice × P/(P − C).
Ore Reserve
The economically mineable part of a Measured or Indicated resource.
Orogenic gold
Gold deposited in shear zones during mountain-building.
Orphan period
The post-discovery phase of the Lassonde curve when share prices sag.
Owner's costs
Costs borne by the project owner (team, studies, insurance) beyond EPC/EPCM.
Oxide ore
Weathered near-surface ore; soft and usually easy to process.

P

P/NAV
Price divided by NAV per share.
Payability
The share of contained metal in concentrate the smelter pays for.
PEA
Preliminary economic assessment (Canadian scoping study).
PFS
Pre-feasibility study (typically ±20–25%).
Placement
Shares issued to selected investors, usually at a discount.
Porphyry
Large, low-grade copper(-gold-molybdenum) deposit formed above an intrusion.
POX
Pressure oxidation in an autoclave to free refractory gold.
Pre-strip
Waste removed before ore production begins (A$47m at Hemi).
Price query
An ASX letter asking a company to explain an unusual price movement.
Probable Reserve
A reserve derived from Indicated (or Measured) resources.
Proved Reserve
A reserve derived from Measured resources.

Q

Qualified Person
The NI 43-101 / S-K 1300 equivalent of a Competent Person.

R

RAB
Rotary air blast drilling: cheap, shallow, contamination-prone.
RC drilling
Reverse circulation drilling: chips return inside the rods; the resource-drilling workhorse.
Real discount rate
A discount rate excluding inflation; use it with real (today's dollar) cash flows.
Reconciliation
Comparing model, grade-control and mill results.
Recovery
Percentage of metal the plant extracts from ore.
Refractory gold
Gold locked in sulphides or otherwise resistant to cyanide.
Risk factor
A multiplier (<1) applied to NPV to reflect stage or jurisdiction risk.
RKEF
Rotary kiln electric furnace processing of saprolite nickel to NPI or matte.
Royalty
A payment based on revenue or profit from a mine (government or private).
RPEEE
Reasonable prospects for eventual economic extraction: the test for reporting a resource.

S

S-K 1300
The US SEC's mining disclosure rules.
SAG mill
Semi-autogenous grinding mill.
Saprolite
Completely weathered clay-rich rock below the laterite.
SC6
Spodumene concentrate at 6% Li₂O.
Scheme of arrangement
A court-approved acquisition needing 75% of votes cast and 50% of holders voting.
Scoping study
The earliest study (±30–50%); may use Inferred resources with cautionary statements.
Shear zone
A zone of intense deformation; a common host for orogenic gold.
Spodumene
A lithium aluminium silicate mineral; the main hard-rock lithium ore.
SPP
Share purchase plan letting retail holders buy (up to A$30,000) at the placement price.
Step-out
A drill hole testing an extension beyond known mineralisation.
Stope
An underground excavation from which ore is extracted.
Stream
An upfront payment for the right to buy future production at a low ongoing price.
Strip ratio
Waste tonnes per ore tonne.
Supergene
Enrichment near the water table from weathering processes.
Sustaining capex
Capital to maintain current production.
SX-EW
Solvent extraction and electrowinning: produces copper cathode from leach solutions.

T

Tailings
Finely ground waste from processing, stored in tailings facilities.
TC/RC
Treatment and refining charges paid by miners to smelters.
Top-cut
Capping extreme assay values to limit nugget-effect bias.
Tornado chart
A ranked bar chart of NPV sensitivities.
Trading halt
A pause in trading, often before a capital raise or material news.
Transitional ore
Partly weathered ore between oxide and fresh rock.
True width
The actual thickness of a mineralised zone, versus the downhole length.

V

VMS
Volcanogenic massive sulphide: Cu-Zn-Pb-Ag-Au deposits formed at ancient seafloor vents.
VWAP
Volume-weighted average price.

W

Weathering profile
Layered sequence from surface cover to fresh rock.
wmt / dmt
Wet vs dry metric tonnes; concentrate prices are per dmt, freight often per wmt.
