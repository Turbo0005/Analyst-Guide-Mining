How this guide works

◆ Key ideaWhole game first, then spiral

Phase A (5 hours) shows you the whole investment story: how miners behave, the Lassonde curve, and how De Grey's Hemi discovery became a A$5bn takeover. Every later module answers a question you will already have. Geology and metallurgy arrive just in time, when a real document needs them.

  • Spine case: every module adds a page to your Hemi dossier. Pages unlock as you complete modules.
  • Interactives: 33 tools, from a drill-section simulator to a Hemi NPV/NAV model with a tornado chart. All of them are on the Tools page.
  • Practice: checkpoint quizzes (80% = mastered), spaced-repetition flashcards and the "What happened next?" prediction game, scored with Brier calibration.
  • Reference: a deposit-types field guide, 13 commodity one-pagers and a glossary.

Fast track (≈40–45 h)

Modules 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 (quarterly and 5B sections), 8 (payable metal and NSR), 10, 14, 15, 16, 17, 19 and 24, then a mini-capstone: a tear sheet plus an implied-price calculation.

Ongoing habits

Read 3–5 quarterlies a week. Track gold, copper, the AUD and one other commodity. Log every call in a decision journal. Play five game rounds a week. Spend 10 minutes a day on flashcards.
